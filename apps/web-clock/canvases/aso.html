Marketing angles — off the store, on the feed

The listing stays clean; the content does the cultural work. Four angles, each with its first clip, the evidence it rides, and the line it does not cross. All run as the same edit across IG Reels, YT Shorts, and TikTok; founder as subject; every comment answered within ~2 hours.

Angle 1 · the hero workout

"The workout he says he does — 27 rounds. I'm starting at 3. Thirty days."

Men's Journal (07-30): "I think my record is 27 rounds… Think you can beat Holland's record?" The challenge and the number already exist in mainstream press; the clip reports them the way that article did.

content onlynever on store / icon / in-app

Legal posture: commentary on a publicly reported workout, no likeness, no film stills, no logo. Founder decides comfort. "27" is a number and the in-app target.

Angle 2 · lock-in season

"Lock-in season starts Oct 1. Every lock-in app is a checkbox. This one has a camera."

"Lock In" 26,542 ratings in 14 months · LOCKED 5,056 · Reload 1,262 · two "Winter Arc" apps shipped this year. The phrase is live and the store proves the wallet. None of them can verify the work.

Sep tease · Oct 1 cohort"lock in" in subtitle

First clip: the checkbox app on the left, our calendar on the right — a day lights only when the camera saw it.

Angle 3 · day-N progress

"Day 1: 3 rounds. Day 30: 11. My phone counted every one."

Founder-as-subject. One 40-minute session every week yields 8–15 distinct clips (day-N cards, the first Rx pull-up, the no-count moment). Cheap, repeatable, and the card carries it so the torso never has to.

The challenge — what the product must ship

Rules simple enough to say in a comment. Everything below is expressible in the event-sourced core; see architecture.md.

30

days, one workout

The 20-minute 5-10-15 every session. Same clock, same three moves, so day 30 compares to day 1 honestly.

5 / 7

days counted

Two rest days a week are part of the rule, not a failure. A day is lit only when the camera counted a finished 20:00.

Scaled

counts

Jumping pull-ups, knee push-ups, box squats are detector variants. Pick on day 1; move to Rx any day. The card says which.

3 → 11

is the score

Day 1 rounds vs day 30 rounds, plus an optional target (default 27). Share a card, not a rank: no public leaderboard in v1.

Trust surface · every direction

Visible NOT COUNTED with a reason, and a manual +1 · it was clean always in reach. Undercount when unsure; never freeze silently. This is the WSFU 1★ pattern inverted.

Never

Ads. Audio ducking. A destructive control in the bottom third during a live set. History that an update can wipe. Footage leaving the phone. Film IP anywhere on the product.

Money

Free: the live count, today's card, the 30-day calendar. Pro: history across arcs, PBs, the replay export, more targets. Annual ~$19.99 and lifetime ~$29.99. Hard paywall + 17–32 day trial only at the reprice outcome.

Differentiation · Apple 4.3 in one sentence

A fixed-length bodyweight challenge whose completion is verified by on-device Vision detectors for one three-movement benchmark — not a habit tracker, not a timer skin, not an N-exercise AI counter.

Strongest reason it's wrong

Trend-shaped twice over (a film and a season). If the native module, three detectors, scaled variants and Rank 0 are not live before Oct 1, we run the cohort late into a lane whose checkbox incumbents already have 26k ratings and a head start.

Falsifier · 30 posts

Non-zero views with installs per 1,000 views ≈ 0; or the modal comment is about the actor rather than the workout; or beginners ask for a program, not a counter.

Identity

Lock-in aesthetic: near-black, one volt accent, big numerals. Not skip-hero's cyan nocturne, not a red-and-blue anything.

Palette

0F1013 16171C D4FF3F volt EEF0F4 FF5F4A reject

Volt is the only colour that means "counted." Reject is the only other colour on a live screen.

Type · icon · voice

Syne 800 for the display and every number; Inter body; IBM Plex Mono for labels and the calendar.

Icon: thirty ticks around a "30", nine lit. It is the calendar, not a spider, not a suit.

Voice: second person, short, no guilt. Say counted, lit, day, rounds, scaled, Rx, card. Don't say grind, shred, AI, beast, cheat day.

IP rules · permanent

No Marvel / Sony / performer names, likenesses, stills, webs, or red-and-blue trade dress on any owned surface. No CrossFit® or 75 Hard® in metadata. "Cindy" allowed in keywords/description, never as brand. "Hero" is our only nod and carries the WOD sense too.

Identity: codename web-clock · bundle com.salaseviciusm.suitup · scheme suitup.

Founder calls before we build the UI

1
Name — default Suit Up. Alts on the board: Round One (warmer, taken as a generic term), Proof of Work (the wedge as a name; reads crypto), Thirty (clean, unsearchable). "Lock In" itself is taken at 26k.
2
Cohort date — default Oct 1. Winter-arc convention. It is also the deadline that decides whether v1 includes scaled detectors (jumping pull-up, knee push-up) or ships Rx-only with a manual scaled mode.
3
How far the hero angle goes in content — default: report the public quote, nothing else. Name and number as commentary, no stills, no likeness, no film audio. Your comfort sets the line; the listing never depends on it.
4
Free calendar or paid calendar — default free. A challenge whose proof is behind a paywall produces no cards and no clean funnel read. Pro is history across arcs and the export.
