# web-clock — Four listing directions

**2026-09-05.** Markdown twin of `canvases/directions.html`. Same engine in all four (see
`architecture.md`); what changes is the **name, store face, palette, first video, and
one HUD surface**. The founder picks a direction; the brand pack is regenerated from it.

Codename stays `web-clock` (stage 1 codename, not a brand). The earlier public name
"Web Clock" is withdrawn.

Rules applied from `playbooks/aso.md` and `docs/marketing/platforms/paid/aso.md`: title
≤ 30 with brand + one category phrase; subtitle ≤ 30 as a benefit, not stuffing;
keyword field 100 chars, comma-separated, no spaces, no plurals, no words already in
title/subtitle; frame 1 sells the one interaction; preview autoplays muted; no
CrossFit® in metadata; no film IP anywhere.

---

## Founder decision (2026-09-05, same day)

**D — the 30-day workout challenge — chosen**, carrying B's off-store marketing angle
(the hero workout / 27 rounds) and a lock-in-season hook. The merged listing is
`canvases/aso.html` and `aso.md`; the working name is **Suit Up**. Store data pulled after
the pick reinforces it: the "lock in" lane is real and recent (Lock In 26,542 ratings
since 2025-06, LOCKED 5,056, 75 Days Challenge 8,662, 75 Hard 6,406) and none of those
apps can verify the work. The recommendation below is kept as the record of what was
argued before the pick.

## Recommendation (pre-decision, for the record)

**Pick B — Rooftop — for the store face and the distribution pilot. Name A — Twenty —
now as the fallback that fires on the *Reposition* outcome** (`market-check.md` §6).

Why B: it is the only direction whose audience is assembled *right now* by something
we did not pay for (a $2.04bn film and a mainstream-press "beat 27 rounds" challenge);
it has the cleanest muted 5-second reveal (clock, count, card vs 27); it produces a
shareable artifact by construction (Q5); and it is the only one that teaches the
**challenge template** the founder wants to reuse for pace and skip-hero. Its weakness
(novelty intent, weak subscription) is priced in: the pilot is buying a distribution
read, not revenue.

Why not the others first: **A** is the best *revenue* audience but has the smallest
reach and already owns a clock. **C** is the best *product truth* — and it lives inside
all four as the trust mechanic — but a wrong no-rep on camera before we have an
accuracy figure would burn the one high-trust community. **D** is the biggest lane
and the wrong fight for a studio without coaching content.

## A · Twenty — the benchmark, counted

- **Name:** Twenty. Alts: *Benchmark*, *Rounds*.
- **Title (27):** `Twenty: Cindy AMRAP Counter`
- **Subtitle (28):** `Camera counts. Clock scores.`
- **Keywords (≤100):** `pullup,pushup,squat,wod,rounds,timer,rep,bodyweight,benchmark,emom,hiit,strict,vision,history,pb`
- **Palette:** Carbon `#0B0B0C` · Signal `#FFD400` · Bone `#F2EFE6` · Ash `#6F6F73`
- **Type:** Barlow Condensed 700 (numerals, captions) · Inter (body)
- **Icon:** black square, oversized yellow `20`, thin arc for the clock.
- **Screenshots:**
  1. *Your Cindy. Counted.* — live HUD: 14:37, ROUND 8, PULL-UPS 3/5, skeleton.
  2. *Prop it. Step back. Go.* — framing gate, bar + body in frame, green START.
  3. *5 · 10 · 15. It advances when you do.* — three-row circuit, push-ups active.
  4. *Rounds + leftovers. The real score.* — 14 + 7 card with per-move totals.
  5. *Same clock. Beat Tuesday.* — history bars, PB 16 + 3.
- **Description, above the fold:** The 20-minute 5-10-15, counted by the camera and
  scored the way the benchmark is scored. Prop the phone, step back, go. Rounds and
  leftovers, no tapping, no ads, history that survives updates.
- **Voice:** gym-floor plain. Say *rounds, leftover, Rx, scaled, bar*. Don't say *AI*,
  *smart*, *revolutionary*.
- **Audience & why they buy:** they already pay for SmartWOD Premium and are furious
  about ads and lost history (`market-check.md` §2). We are the clock that also counts.
- **Strongest reason it's wrong:** the box already has a clock and a judge; a strict
  detector will no-rep kipping pull-ups → needs Rx/scaled toggle on day one.
- **Falsifier:** 30 posts, modal comment is "my box has a clock" / "my watch does this."

- **Icon:** indigo gradient, black skyline silhouette along the bottom edge, ember

- **Screenshots:**
  1. *20 minutes. Three moves. One number.* — live HUD, 12:04, ROUND 9, ember count.
  2. *Prop your phone. Step back.* — framing gate over a night-lit room; ember START.
  3. *It counts. You climb.* — round ticker climbing 7 → 8 → 9 with the clock.
  4. *Your number vs 27.* — card: **9 + 12**, a target bar with 27 at the end.
  5. *Post the card, not the video.* — skeleton-replay share card, no face, no torso.
- **Description, above the fold:** The 20-minute hero workout: five pull-ups, ten
  push-ups, fifteen squats, as many rounds as you can. The camera counts every rep
  and every round. Set your target. Beat it. Share the card, not your face.
- **Voice:** late-night, second person, short. Say *rounds, target, card, climb*.
  Never *Spider-Man, Holland, web, Marvel*. "Hero" carries both senses (hero WOD /
  hero training) and is our only nod.
- **Audience & why they install:** they arrived from a clip with a number in their
  head. The listing's job is to promise the card in frame 1 and frame 4.

  scaled toggle so a beginner still gets a number.
- **Strongest reason it's wrong:** trend-shaped; 16–25 male skew resents paywalls;
  if the app ships after interest decays we have a dark-mode counter with a skyline.
- **Falsifier:** 30 posts, non-zero views, installs per 1,000 views ≈ 0, or the modal
  comment is about the film rather than the workout.
- **Legal posture:** store surfaces carry no film IP. Content may reference the
  publicly reported workout and number as commentary; founder decides comfort.

- **Title (26):** `Strict: Camera-Judged Reps`

- **Keywords (≤100):** `pullup,pushup,squat,amrap,cindy,counter,form,rounds,timer,bodyweight,calisthenics,chin,lockout`
- **Palette:** Paper `#F4F1EA` · Ink `#111111` · Stamp `#D62828` · Pencil `#8A8578`
- **Type:** Archivo Black (display) · IBM Plex Mono (data)
- **Icon:** paper square, red rubber-stamp `NO REP` at a slight tilt.
- **Screenshots:**
  1. *Chin over bar or it didn't happen.* — HUD with rep list: ✓ ✓ ✓ ✗ ✓.
  2. *Frame the bar. Then we start.* — framing gate, ink outline, red until honest.
  3. *NO REP · lockout short.* — full-frame stamp with the reason line.
  4. *Counted 63. Rejected 6.* — score card with rejected count and reasons.
  5. *Argue with the replay.* — per-rep skeleton scrub with the joint angle.
- **Description, above the fold:** A rep counter that says no. Chin over the bar, elbows
  locked, hips below parallel — or it isn't counted, and it tells you why. The
  20-minute 5-10-15, judged live by the camera, on your phone, offline.
- **Voice:** referee. Terse. Say *no rep, lockout, depth, chin, judged*. Never *cheat*
  about the user; the stamp is about the rep.
- **Audience & why they install:** GOLDEN's "easy to cheat" review is the demand
  statement; calisthenics people already argue about "was that a real rep?"
- **Product delta:** reason codes on every rejection, per-rep replay, published
  accuracy sheet in-app (we have none yet — `[GAP]`).
- **Strongest reason it's wrong:** a wrong no-rep on camera reads as a broken app to
  the harshest judges on the store; we have no accuracy figure to stand on.
- **Falsifier:** "the app is broken" comments on reps the founder can show were legit.

## What is shared across all four

- **Frame order:** 1 live HUD · 2 framing gate · 3 the circuit/judge beat · 4 the
  score card · 5 the return loop. Frame 1 and 4 carry the install.
- **Preview (10 s, muted):** prop (0–1) · gate green (1–2) · pull-ups 1→5 (2–5) ·
  advance with clock (5–8) · card (8–10). On-screen text only.
- **Monetization:** free live count + today's card; Pro = history/PB/target
  progression/replay export; annual ~$19.99 and lifetime ~$29.99. Flip to hard
  paywall + 17–32 day trial only at the *Reprice* outcome.
- **Trust surface:** visible "not counted" + manual +1 in every direction.
- **Never:** ads, audio ducking, a public leaderboard in v1, film IP, CrossFit® in
  metadata.
