Thirty days. Twenty minutes a day. Five pull-ups, ten push-ups, fifteen squats, as many rounds as you can. Knee push-ups and jumping pull-ups count. The camera counts every rep — so a day is only lit when you actually did it. Day 30 is a card: your number vs day 1. No account. No ads. Nothing leaves your phone.

THE WORKOUT
One benchmark, every day: 5 pull-ups, 10 push-ups, 15 air squats, on repeat for twenty minutes. Gyms call it Cindy. Your score is rounds. Your progress is day 30 against day 1.

THE CAMERA COUNTS
Prop your phone, step back, and Suit Up watches your reps with on-device body tracking. Each rep is counted the moment it is done — or shown as not counted, with the reason: chin below the bar, chest short, no lockout. If it was clean and the camera missed it, tap +1. Honest reps, honest score.

START WHERE YOU ARE
Pick your version on day 1: jumping pull-ups or pull-ups, knee push-ups or push-ups, box squats or air squats. Every version counts and is named on your card. Move up to Rx when you are ready.

THIRTY DAYS YOU CAN'T FAKE
The calendar lights a day only when the camera saw a finished twenty minutes. Five days a week is the rule — rest is a rule, not a failure. There is no box to tick.

POST THE CARD, NOT YOUR FACE
Day 30 gives you a card: rounds on day 1, rounds on day 30, days counted, your version. A body outline drawn from keypoints — never the video. Share it anywhere.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
The camera runs on your iPhone and only on your iPhone. Frames are read for body keypoints and discarded. Nothing is recorded, stored, or uploaded. No account, no ads, no subscription.

Requires an iPhone with iOS 17 or later and a place to prop it.
