# Suit Up — Privacy Policy

_Effective: on first App Store release. Host this page at a public URL and paste that URL
into App Store Connect → App Privacy → Privacy Policy URL._

Suit Up is a workout challenge app that counts your reps with your iPhone's camera.

## What the camera does

The camera runs only while you are in a session and only on your device. Each frame is
read by Apple's on-device Vision framework to find body keypoints (shoulders, elbows,
wrists, hips, knees, ankles) and is then discarded. **No photo or video is ever recorded,
stored, or transmitted.** The keypoints from one frame of each session are kept on your
device to draw the outline on your share card.

## What is stored on your device

Your sessions (rounds, reps counted, reps not counted and why, the version you chose), your
challenge calendar, and your settings. This lives in a database inside the app on your
iPhone. Deleting the app deletes it; you can also erase it from Settings → Erase all data.

## What leaves your device

Nothing personal. There is no account. If analytics are enabled for a build, the app sends
anonymous product-interaction events (for example: "a session finished with 4 rounds") to
our analytics provider so we can tell whether the app works. These events are not linked
to your identity and are not used for tracking or advertising. The camera frames, your
keypoints, and your share cards are never sent anywhere by the app; a card is only shared
where you choose to share it via the iOS share sheet.

## Children

Suit Up is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this URL with a new effective
