# Marketing research — App Factory

Kicked off 2026-08-05 by the founder. Assumption throughout: **bootstrapped**, no VC
money, near-zero paid budget until something proves out, founder supplies raw demo
recordings and AI agents turn them into content.

## Structure

- `platforms/<platform>/algorithm.md` — how distribution actually works, claims tagged
  `[DOCUMENTED]` vs `[ANECDOTAL]` with sources
- `platforms/<platform>/audience.md` — which niches/audiences concentrate there, and
  which app categories fit
- `platforms/<platform>/case-studies.md` — real apps, real numbers, wins **and**
  failures, plus "where are they now"
- `platforms/<platform>/credibility.md` — posting at volume without reading as spam
- `failures/` — cross-platform post-mortems, a reusable failure taxonomy, and the
  realistic bootstrapped baseline (survivorship bias called out)
- `pipeline/` — tooling survey, end-to-end content pipeline design, self-improving
  feedback loop, and ToS/ban risks
- `synthesis/` — the Wave 3 conclusions: what to do, per platform and per app

## Research waves — all complete (2026-08-05)

- **Wave 1 ✅** — tiktok · instagram · youtube · communities (Reddit/HN/PH/IH) ·
  x-linkedin · paid + ASO
- **Wave 2 ✅** — failures + bootstrapped baseline · content pipeline + feedback loop
- **Wave 3 ✅** — synthesis (indexed below)

## Synthesis — start here

- [`synthesis/ranking.md`](synthesis/ranking.md) — cross-platform ranking for a zero-budget,
  screen-recording app factory. **#1 IG Reels + YT Shorts, #2 TikTok, #3 YT long-form +
  third-party reviews**; explicit don't-bother-yet tier (Reddit-at-volume, X/LinkedIn, HN/PH,
  paid). The App Store listing is rank 0 — the precondition for all of it.
- [`synthesis/platform-selection.md`](synthesis/platform-selection.md) — a six-question
  decision procedure keyed off category/audience/monetisation, with worked examples for
  pullup, running-with-pace, and skip-hero.
- [`synthesis/playbook.md`](synthesis/playbook.md) — the week-to-week operating rhythm: the
  founder's ~40-min recording burden, what agents do unattended, cadence per platform, the
  engagement loop, the gates that unlock paid, and the kill/pivot triggers.
- [`synthesis/open-questions.md`](synthesis/open-questions.md) — what the research could not
  establish, and the four real-world experiments that would settle it.
